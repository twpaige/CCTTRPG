RoughWriter — Quick Guide

Advance. No retreat. No surrender. Write. RoughWriter helps you practice getting words down without stopping to revise.

Get started

Choose / Reconnect Folder in the footer to select a local writing folder and grant write access. Click Begin Session and start typing. Your writing is saved as plain text in the folder you choose.

Keep moving

You cannot edit earlier text during a session. Press Enter after writing to commit a segment; press Enter twice to end the session. The visible-buffer slider controls how much recent text stays readable. Older text is visually scrambled, not deleted.

Pauses and saving

The idle-timeout slider sets how long you can pause before the session ends automatically. RoughWriter saves the original session text during writing and at the end. It also keeps a temporary browser recovery copy for interrupted work. If saving fails, use Retry Save or Copy to preserve your words.

Where your writing goes

Sessions are stored as timestamped text files. Completed sessions are appended to the selected master .txt document, verified, and the individual files are moved to Sessions/Archived. The master selector in the footer lets you use an existing master or create a new one between sessions. Your files remain on your own computer or synced folder.

Archive and records

Use Archive to search and read individual sessions, or copy their full text. Delete permanently removes only that individual session file after confirmation; text already appended to the master remains. The statistics show your current session and personal bests.
