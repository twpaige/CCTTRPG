Crown & Call

You remember your life.

Your family. Your friends. Your work. Everything was ordinary—until the men in green suits came.

You were dragged before twelve elderly judges and condemned for crimes you had not yet committed. Your sentence: banishment to Origin—for this life and every life thereafter, for the eternity of your being.

A flash of light.
Then wilderness.

Welcome to Origin

Crown & Call is a persistent roleplaying MUD set in an enormous frontier world built by the Banished themselves.

For more than two centuries, people like you have built towns, farms, roads, churches, shops and outposts across a wilderness where civilization once did not exist. Technology has settled into an early-to-mid-1800s frontier existence.

And death won't get you out.

Your body -your vessel- can die. You cannot. Your soul eventually returns in another vessel, carrying the memories and knowledge of previous lives. But Origin has customs of its own: a new vessel means a new life. Old property, grudges and relationships are expected to remain behind.

Usually.

Crown & Call combines vast procedurally generated landscapes with traditional, handcrafted MUD locations and interiors. The wilderness and authored world exist together: explore continuous terrain, then walk through the door of an individually built tavern, homestead, church or sprawling settlement.

Travel tens of thousands of miles across mountain ranges and open plains, follow rivers through wilderness, cross vast seas and discover places other players may never have seen.

Hunt. Craft. Trade. Explore. Build relationships. Make enemies.

Join civilization—or leave it behind.

Gather your friends, disappear beyond the frontier and build a life and community of your own. Create traditions, rivalries and stories nobody planned for you.

Origin has mysteries.
You can pursue them—or ignore them completely.

This is your life. Go live it.
