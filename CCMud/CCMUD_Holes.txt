ASSESSMENT
==========

CCMUD has a substantial design foundation. The major missing work is not
another long interview about small object interactions. It is completing
a few playable system contracts and reconciling incompatible records.

This audit groups the meaningful remaining work into TEN areas:
H01 Combat and rule integration
H02 Movement, stamina, and carrying capacity
H03 The complete starter crafting and survival chain
H04 Wildlife and renewable-resource persistence
H05 Perception, illumination, and information boundaries
H06 Origin's NPC interactions and basic economy
H07 Persistent-world recovery and safe updates
H08 Return-from-death eligibility and state
H09 Property ownership and access lifecycle
H10 Advancement and capability content

H01-H08 affect the promised first playable experience or preservation of its
history. They can be closed with deliberately small initial specifications;
they do not require every future feature. H09 is needed before full property
systems launch. H10 is needed before meaningful long-term progression.

This is a bounded list of work areas, NOT ten mandatory interview questions
and NOT a claim that every unspecified value requires Thomas's decision.
Many remaining values are content authoring or playtest configuration.


H01 — COMBAT AND RULE INTEGRATION
================================
Priority: Before the hunting/combat prototype.
Type: Missing integration decisions; conflicting source procedures.

Already settled:
Combat is turn-based and spatial. Engagements are open rather than private
instances. Forced attacks and ranged participation are allowed. The ruleset
already supplies opposed rolls, weapon ceilings, armor, wound combination,
defenses, conditions, and much of magic. Do not reinvent that foundation.

The hole:
W section 13 proposes declarations followed by server resolution; R T5-T7
uses side-based initiative, ordered Turns, HOLD, and reactive PROTECT. A
server cannot apply both procedures without an explicit translation.

R T3 uses five-foot squares, adjacency, and Base Speed + ATHLETICS. D Q57
uses continuous travel modes and two time/speed multipliers; D Q60 applies
wound speed penalties, while R C5 says wounds do not reduce Speed. The
documents do not establish the combat/world boundary for these rules.

The clock contract is also incomplete. Human declaration time, represented
seven-second rounds, the 6x world clock, and real-time stamina regeneration
coexist. Without a rule, waiting to submit an action could change stamina,
illumination, healing, or positions while very little combat time passes.
Deferring the large reinforcement envelope does not remove this local issue.

Minimum closure:
- Choose the initial declaration/order procedure, deadline, default action
  when a player is silent/disconnected, and when declarations become final.
- Define coordinate melee reach, movement allowance, collision/cover, and
  the point at which attack range and defenses are evaluated.
- Define combat entry/exit and which actions reset the existing aggression
  timer. Distinguish that logout timer from the ruleset's Aggression value.
- State which clocks drive combat stamina, bleeding, healing, and effects.
- Record which tabletop procedures are adopted, adapted, or staff-only.
  Include the wound-name mapping: D Q60's Light/Moderate/Minor/Major/Grave/
  Mortal is not the same vocabulary as R's FLSH/LITE/DEEP/SEVR/GRAV/MORT.

Completion example:
A player throws a rock at a moving rabbit, receives no further input for a
round, reconnects, and finishes or leaves the engagement with one unambiguous
position, wound state, ammunition outcome, and aggression expiry.

References: D Q15, Q47-Q50, Q57-Q60, Q67; W section 13;
R C5, W1-W11, T1-T8, O1.
The causal travel envelope and full PvP balancing remain later work.


H02 — MOVEMENT, STAMINA, AND CARRYING CAPACITY
============================================
Priority: Before general travel and inventory are considered playable.
Type: Direct contradiction plus explicitly unfinished balance specification.

Already settled:
Movement speeds, multiplicative modifiers, stop-on-obstruction behavior,
no automatic routing, and stamina regeneration are specified. Carrying
weight is recursive. Bulk is deliberately not a general simulation.

The hole:
D Q45 says height, weight, and build have zero mechanical effect and weight
is not tracked as a separate field. D Q61 nevertheless calculates capacity
from body weight. There is no stated reference weight that resolves this.
Using prose weight would allow descriptive choices to change capability.

Stamina expenditure for each movement mode is still missing. Knowing the
recovery formula alone cannot determine sprint duration, pursuit, or whether
JOG remains sustainable at a particular ATHLX. W's older "no stamina cost"
for WALK and D Q58's slower recovery while walking also need a single
current interpretation.

Minimum closure:
Resolve the weight contradiction explicitly, then author one small movement
cost table with units and reference endurance cases. Retain the settled
recovery formula and speed hierarchy. Define only the initial physical
crossings actually offered; D Q71 explicitly leaves swimming, falling, and
related costs/severity to implementation. If enabled, those actions need
outcomes before they can cause persistent deaths.

Completion example:
At ATHLX 0 and 20, a known load gives a predictable capacity, WALK speed,
sprint duration, exhaustion stop, and recovery time.

References: D Q45, Q57-Q63, Q71-Q72, Q148; W "Movement Speed & Stamina".
Exact terrain, weather, and altitude curves are tunable implementation work,
not reasons to reopen the accepted movement model.


H03 — THE COMPLETE STARTER CRAFTING AND SURVIVAL CHAIN
====================================================
Priority: Before the minimum private-alpha loop can be completed.
Type: Missing content specification, not a missing crafting architecture.

Already settled:
Known Crafts succeed deterministically. Execution validates and commits
atomically, produces the result immediately, and adds Work Timer debt.
Learning takes real time, one Craft at a time. Materials must be physically
available. Hunger/thirst are slow; shelter and sleep are not survival meters.

The hole:
There is no complete dependency list connecting starting equipment and
opening Crafts to a rock, bow, arrows, rabbit processing, fire, cooked food,
tent, map, and compass. D Q38 explicitly leaves the openers unspecified;
D Q21 and Q26 leave outfitting and compass ingredients unspecified.

The chain must work without a circular prerequisite such as needing a
manufactured tool to obtain the only material used to manufacture that tool.
Normal town starts and deliberately difficult Naked remote starts need
separate checks. Remote does not mean easy, but its route to progress must
exist if it is offered.

Minimum closure:
Author a compact table for the required starter actions: prerequisites,
tools, input quantities, output objects/quantities, Work Time, and Learning
Time where relevant. Include full hands, physical worksite access, tent
deployment/packing, and the means to obtain initial tools and navigation.
Specify the first food/water replenishment values and fire fuel/extinction
behavior. Detailed nutrition and fire-spread simulation are unnecessary.

Completion example:
Trace Normal and Naked starts to their first edible meal and usable shelter,
then trace the map/compass/tent tutorial without an unexplained item grant or
staff intervention. Show where the Work Timer can interrupt the chain.

References: D Q1-Q6, Q12, Q21, Q26, Q38, Q66, Q69; W sections 4 and 12.
A broad Craft-versus-recipe system need not be settled to author this chain.


H04 — WILDLIFE AND RENEWABLE-RESOURCE PERSISTENCE
===============================================
Priority: Before repeatable hunting and gathering.
Type: Missing state/lifecycle contract.

Already settled:
Geography is deterministic, nature need not be instantiated object by object,
and meaningful changes persist. Resource types are authored rather than
invented by the generator. Hunting rabbits is mandatory for the first loop.

The hole:
Those principles do not yet define when a rabbit becomes an individual,
how it moves/flees and reacts to attacks, what survives when an area sleeps,
or how local hunting/gathering changes later availability.

D Q85 explicitly leaves depletion/regrowth persistence for later. W explains
how chopping creates logs and a stump but not the amount that can be removed
from a locality before availability changes. A player revisiting or waking
an area must not accidentally get a fresh resource supply solely because
a cache was discarded.

Minimum closure:
Define a small rabbit behavior/stat profile and carcass transformation,
plus local renewable-resource availability, consumption, and replenishment.
State which animal state is retained after interaction and which untouched
population detail can be reconstructed. Specify only the resources used by
the initial chain; a continent-wide ecological simulation is not required.

Completion example:
Two players hunt/gather in the same location; the area sleeps and wakes.
Existing animals, carcasses, harvested supplies, and local availability have
consistent results rather than duplicated or reset outcomes.

References: D Q1, Q4-Q5, Q10, Q85; W sections 2-3 and 6-7;
W world-generation Q126-Q151.


H05 — PERCEPTION, ILLUMINATION, AND INFORMATION BOUNDARIES
========================================================
Priority: Before reliable exploration and hunting.
Type: Partly specified system needing a minimum executable policy.

Already settled:
SENSE, opposed concealment, separate LOOK/SCAN/SEARCH/TRACK, physical search,
continuous illumination/falloff, fixed observation cadence, private labels,
and BRIEF's presentation-only behavior are established. Numeric perception
bands in W are explicitly illustrative.

The hole:
There is no compact mapping from object size/light/cover/distance and SENSE
to what is visible, targetable, or readable in the initial world. The five-
second observation cadence does not itself supply that rule. Day/night
lighting also needs a usable initial calendar/solar model, even if advanced
weather is deferred.

D Q78 prevents NEW labels on concealed identities, but does not explicitly
say whether an existing label remains displayed/targetable while that
identity is concealed. This is an information-boundary ambiguity, not a
request for an elaborate disguise system.

Minimum closure:
Author a small initial perception/light/sound configuration and clear
eligibility rules for target resolution. Preserve the distinction between
seeing a light and seeing by it, and between hearing speech and seeing its
accompanying emote. Resolve existing-label behavior under concealment.
Ensure movement collision/attack validation is not limited to the slower
player-facing observation cadence.

Completion example:
A rabbit in daylight, a lantern at night, an object behind a closed door,
and a previously labeled hooded character produce consistent LOOK, targeting,
and communication results. BRIEF must reveal the same permitted facts.

References: D Q43-Q44, Q73-Q85, Q99-Q100, Q170-Q176, Q219-Q222;
W sections 8-10 and 14; W "Travel Observation" and "WATCH".
Exact light equations and every possible sound range can be tuned later.


H06 — ORIGIN'S NPC INTERACTIONS AND BASIC ECONOMY
===============================================
Priority: Before the promised town jobs/sell-game/buy-beer loop.
Type: Missing initial gameplay/content contract.

Already settled:
Origin is hand placed, has about 200 NPCs and named service categories, and
NPCs keep businesses functioning. Coins and commerce are required. Introductory
jobs are optional and late returns are not penalized.

The hole:
The documents describe the experience without specifying the first job's
acceptance/completion state, rewards, trade interface, prices, currency units,
or how a merchant remains stocked and able to buy hunted goods. "NPCs run
shops" does not decide whether stock and money are finite, replenished, or
abstracted.

Minimum closure:
Define the initial currency unit/rounding and a small purchase/sale table;
state merchant stock/cash behavior; author one delivery and one hunting or
gathering job with persistent progress and payout rules. Specify the small
interaction vocabulary or dialogue approach needed to obtain these services.
A complete economic simulation or individually simulated daily schedule
for all 200 NPCs is not necessary.

Completion example:
An ordinary new character accepts work, disconnects, returns late, delivers
the goods once, sells a carcass/product, and buys a beer without losing or
duplicating the reward.

References: D Q1-Q5, Q9, Q23, Q27-Q28.
Player-owned shop profits, business resale, and settlement economies are
later features rather than blockers for this initial contract.


H07 — PERSISTENT-WORLD RECOVERY AND SAFE UPDATES
==============================================
Priority: Before persistent production play.
Type: Missing operational/game-state contract.

Already settled:
Persistent changes are authoritative; caches are disposable; crafting is
atomic; timestamps support offline progress; initial player history should
survive into later releases. The server, not the browser, owns game state.

The hole:
Atomic crafting does not specify crash behavior for movement, combat, money,
inventory transfers, or whole-world recovery. The docs do not set acceptable
data loss, backup/restore expectations, or whether server downtime advances
world-dependent conditions. Ordinary disconnect rules cannot automatically
be assumed to describe a dead server.

Changes to a generation algorithm are another risk: reconstructing the same
coordinates with different geography could move a river through a persisted
cabin or detach a bookmarked feature from what the player recorded.

Minimum closure:
Define restart outcomes for position, engagement, wounds, timers, inventory,
and in-flight purchases/transfers. State the backup/restore objective and
what players are told if a rollback occurs. Specify how the seed and relevant
generation/content versions are retained and how persistent areas survive
generator changes. Choose a simple transaction/retry policy consistent with
"one action, one result."

Completion example:
A controlled server interruption during the core loop recovers to a coherent
state; a cache eviction and a generator update do not erase the tent, reset
harvesting history, or silently relocate known geography.

References: D Q5, Q9-Q10, Q47-Q53, Q85; W sections 3, 6-7, 12 and 15.
Database schema, chunk size, cache expiry, and deployment tooling are
engineering choices. The player-visible recovery promises are the design gap.
W world-generation Q219 also explicitly leaves individual geography-edit
reversion unanswered; this can remain deferred if a minimal recovery policy
does not depend on that feature.


H08 — RETURN-FROM-DEATH ELIGIBILITY AND STATE
============================================
Priority: Before player death can occur in persistent play.
Type: Explicit unresolved eligibility and related lifecycle ambiguity.

Already settled — DO NOT REOPEN:
No playable PC corpse remains. Carried/worn possessions drop immediately at
the death coordinates and are immediately lootable. Stored possessions stay
where they are. D Q247 was withdrawn because these facts were already covered.

The hole:
D Q25 defines one lifetime accidental/environmental reprieve and mandatory
Naked 10000 after murder. D Q34 introduces a reduced-CAP return and explicitly
says eligibility needs reconciliation. D Q149 adds death-area administrative
review and possible return at Origin without explaining the complete relation
to the earlier routes.

The record also does not give a consolidated rule for whether a returning
same character retains learned Crafts, an active learning commitment, banked
tokens, work debt, and the automatic-progression cutoff. Inheritance is
triggered by death, so the effect of returning on an already-started succession
also needs to be stated, not inferred.

Minimum closure:
Write ONE eligibility/state table for the existing return routes. Preserve
the murder restriction unless Thomas explicitly changes it. State how return
interacts with the lifetime reprieve and pending inheritance; do not assume
return automatically reverses completed transfers. Treat R F6's tabletop
Petition for the Dead as an integration question, not an automatic extra
CCMUD respawn path.

Completion example:
An accidental death before and after the lifetime reprieve, and a murdered
character's return, each have one allowed set of choices and clear retained
state without changing the settled corpse/loot rules.

References: D Q25, Q34, Q41, Q149-Q156; R F6.
This is a narrow reconciliation task, not a new death-and-corpses interview.


H09 — PROPERTY OWNERSHIP AND ACCESS LIFECYCLE
============================================
Priority: Before registered property, succession, or player banking launches.
Type: Incomplete integration with several directly stated open cases.

Already settled:
Deeds convey ownership. Registered doors use character-recognizing magical
permissions. Heir eligibility widens on a specified schedule; eviction,
front-door sale, depreciation, and forfeited contents are substantially
defined. Protected property and unprotected wilderness caches are distinct.

The hole:
D Q152 makes deed possession sufficient for ownership, while D Q154-Q156
uses registered owners, door permissions, heirs, and subsequent sale. The
documents do not explain when these representations synchronize or what
happens to the former deed when an heir or buyer acquires the property.
Storage in a bank lockbox further means "possession" must be defined beyond
simply holding the deed.

The initial creation/registration of a claim, its protected boundary, and
permitted use of stored goods are not fully specified. Opening a door is not
necessarily permission to take everything inside under W's no-theft rule.
D Q154 expressly leaves the relationship to older container/window locks
and lockpicking unsettled.

Minimum closure:
Define one ownership authority and the deed/door transfer process; distinguish
door entry, use of goods, removal, and ownership. Specify claim registration
and scope before enabling claims. Complete the lifecycle cases already named
as unresolved: no-heir timing, unregistered-claim contest response, and rental
reversion. State how authorized inheritance/sale replaces prior ownership
rather than leaving competing owners.

Completion example:
A deed stored in a lockbox, an eligible heir at a door, and a later purchaser
cannot produce contradictory owners or stale access authority.

References: D Q131-Q154, Q156-Q160, Q167; W world-generation Q168-Q174.
Do not reopen the settled 30-day heir expansion, 30-day eviction notice, or
compounded sale formula. Currency rounding belongs with H06.


H10 — ADVANCEMENT AND CAPABILITY CONTENT
=======================================
Priority: Before long-term advancement is enabled; starter subset earlier.
Type: Deliberately unfinished tuning/content plus ruleset integration.

Already settled:
CAP token costs, banking, individual caps, the normal total-cap cutoff,
exceptional admin awards, independent learning/work timers, and committed
Craft learning are defined. Meaningful play must matter; raw command spam
must not be advancement currency.

The hole:
The initial earning cadence and qualifying activity policy are still
provisional. The actual opening Crafts, prerequisite data, and language
unlock table are unfinished. D Q174 explicitly identifies an inconsistent
example rather than a final language progression.

R already contains an authored Calling list and FAITH mechanics. Therefore,
the gap is identifying the adopted CCMUD subset, its access rules, and which
GM-dependent decisions require staff versus automatic handling. It is not
evidence that no magic or healing design exists.

Minimum closure:
Publish a provisional, testable advancement policy and the capability/content
tables needed for the enabled features. Cross-reference the existing ruleset
and record CCMUD-specific exceptions, especially time units and recovery.
Restrict initial content deliberately instead of blocking the first playable
loop on a complete future Craft tree.

Completion example:
A player can understand the next available advancement, the next learnable
Craft, and their actual language/Calling access from the same rules the server
uses. Passive account age alone does not guarantee normal advancement.

References: D Q18, Q34-Q40, Q54, Q69, Q169-Q174; W section 12;
R M1-M11, F1-F6, O1, O5-O6.
Writing/publishing-specific expansion is paused and is not requested here.


DOCUMENT RECONCILIATION — NOT NEW DESIGN QUESTIONS
==================================================

These discrepancies should be resolved or annotated when the relevant system
is implemented. An obvious later decision should not be re-interviewed.

1. Walking speed:
   W section 13 still illustrates 4 mph baseline / 96 miles per real hour.
   D Q57 locks 3 mph WALK / 72 miles per real hour. Use the later D value.

2. Offline conveyance consent:
   W's "Group Migration & Offline Passengers" requires prior consent.
   D Q52 explicitly removes separate consent. Preserve the later decision.

3. Physical versus magical door keys:
   D Q154 supersedes earlier physical door-key proposals. Do not implement
   both as competing defaults. Container/window/picking integration remains
   an actual open issue under H09.

4. Geographic names versus property names:
   W world-generation Q205-Q206 permits proposed names for constructed
   landmarks, including buildings/property. D Q166 later prohibits player-
   created custom property names. Treat the latter as the property exception,
   retaining other geographic naming decisions.

5. Written-language recognition:
   D Q170/Q172/Q176 still contain speech-only/no-writing-recognition wording.
   D Q219-Q220 explicitly supersedes it by reusing recognition rules for writing.
   This is already resolved, not a fresh hole.

6. Sleep commentary:
   D Q69 says it supersedes Q54's lack of a healing benefit, but current Q54
   already includes the benefit. This is stale historical wording, not a
   disagreement about whether sleep helps. The rates and clock integration
   with R O1 are separate work under H01/H10.

7. Roads and immutable documents:
   W's no inherent road-speed bonus does not prevent D Q63's improved travel
   surface. W's ordinary historical objects are not immortal; D Q204/Q209
   establishes a later explicit exception for published works. These are
   compatible scoped rules, not contradictions needing new interviews.

8. Stale navigation:
   D calls its own file CCMudDesign.txt under PROJECT REFERENCES, while the
   actual file is CCMUD_Design.txt. Historical bookmarks and the original
   discussion queue must never override the current audit-first bookmark.

9. Ruleset authority:
   D points to W as authoritative, while W says to use Crown & Call directly.
   R contains tabletop procedures and has its own evolution. Record an explicit
   source/version and exception list for implementation. Neither importing
   every tabletop rule nor silently ignoring later CCMUD decisions is adequate.
   H01 and H08 identify the consequences that need actual decisions.


INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED — NOT RECOMMENDED FOR THE NEXT INTERVIEW
==============================================================

- Writing/publishing mechanics: D Q246 remains deferred. Provenance visibility
  and remaining details are unresolved; Thomas may drastically simplify the
  book rules. Preserve D Q181-Q245, including Q197's recorded deferral and the
  later nested-provenance decisions. Do not add book edge-case questions.
- Death/corpses: D Q247 is withdrawn/duplicate. H08 addresses eligibility and
  cross-system state only.
- World metaphysics: D Q14's optional lore is deferred; magical arrival and
  agelessness already have decisions.
- Full PvP, causal reinforcement envelopes, advanced disguise/tracking,
  farming/ranching, mining/excavation, complex weather, advanced construction,
  wagons/boats, player shops, clans/governments, and full staff succession:
  their detailed implementation is outside the minimum under D Q6.
  Several have substantial existing rules; postponement is not absence.
- Future world generation: the reviewed files do not supply a complete
  generator specification for topology, hydrology, or underground geometry.
  Before treating these as new design holes, retrieve any earlier generator
  decisions. W's detailed generation interview begins at Q122; its numbering
  alone is not evidence that Q1-Q121 were never answered. The immediate need
  here is an initial terrain/lighting/resource contract and version stability.
- Cosmetic state priorities, exact ink palette, every tool statistic, pager
  bounds, detailed environmental curves, and cache/chunk tuning are content
  or implementation work, not separate major design interviews.


RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP
=====================

Complete a short "first playable contract" covering H01-H08. Start with the
starter chain and rabbit encounter so missing dependencies become visible,
and resolve the weight and return-route contradictions alongside it.

Use content tables and a few end-to-end examples wherever possible. Ask
Thomas only for choices that change gameplay or resolve incompatible locked
decisions. Do not ask again whether maps are physical, Crafts fail randomly,
characters leave corpses, or the world uses rooms.

Then address H09-H10 when property and progression are scheduled. Keep later
systems parked until their implementation requires them.

This audit is a worklist, not an expansion of the numbered interview. If
Thomas elects to resume that interview, its next new number remains 248.
